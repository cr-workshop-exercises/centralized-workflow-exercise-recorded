# Greek Salad Recipe

## Ingredients
- 2 tomatoes
- 1 cucumber
- 1/2 red onion
100g feta cheese
- handful of olives
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- oregano
- salt and pepper

## Instructions
 - chop the tomatoes and cucumber
- add the red onion, olives and feta
- add the tomatoes, olives, feta and mix everything together
- drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice
- season with oregano salt and mepper
- bake the salad for 20 minutes at 200 degrees
