# Soy yoghurt

## Ingredients

- **2 tbs** soy-joghurt
- **500 ml** soy milk 


## Instructions

1. **Prepare:**
   - Add the ingredients into a mason jar.
   - Stir well until the joghurt is incoporated into the milk.

2. **Instant pot:**
   - Open the instant pot and put the mason jar inside.
   - Close the lid.
   - Turn on the joghurt function and set a timer to 14 hours.

3. **Next day:**
   - After 14 hours your yoghurt should have thickened a bit and turned more tangy.
   - You can close the mason jar and store the yoghurt in the fridge.

4. **Notes**
   - It is important to use soy milk, that does not contain any additives like gum or stabilizers.
   - The yoghurt will not be super thick, more like a joghurt drink.
   - Experiment with sweeteners and other flavours, e.g. vanilla or cinnamon
