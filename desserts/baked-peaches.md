# Recipe for baked peaches

## Ingredients
- peaches
- cinnamon
- butter 
- brown sugar

## Instructions
- Heat the over to 180 degrees C
- Halve the peaches and remove their stones
- Place the halves in a oven disk and spread butter on the halves
- Sprinkle the halves with cinnamon and brown sugar
- Bake for 30 minutes or until caramelized
