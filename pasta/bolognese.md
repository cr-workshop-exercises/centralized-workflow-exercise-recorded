# Bolognese recipe


## Ingredients

- 1 pound ground beef (or a mix of beef and pork)
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 carrot, finely diced
- 1 celery stalk, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/4 cup tomato paste
- 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 cup red wine
- 1/2 cup whole milk
- 1/2 cup beef or chicken stock
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- Fresh basil or parsley (optional, for garnish)


## Instructions

1. **Sauté the Soffritto:**
   - Heat the olive oil in a large Dutch oven or heavy pot over medium heat.
   - Add the onion, carrot, and celery. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are softened and the onion is translucent, about 8–10 minutes.
   - Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

2. **Brown the Meat:**
   - Add the ground beef (or beef and pork mix) and cook, breaking it up with a spoon, until no pink remains, about 8–10 minutes.
   - Season with salt and pepper.

3. **Add Tomato Paste and Wine:**
   - Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes.
   - Pour in the red wine and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Let the wine reduce by about half, 3–5 minutes.

4. **Add Tomatoes and Simmer:**
   - Add the crushed tomatoes, stock, milk, and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low.
   - Cover partially and simmer gently, stirring occasionally, for at least 2 hours (3 hours is ideal for a richer, deeper flavor). The sauce should thicken and darken as it cooks.

5. **Season and Serve:**
   - Remove the bay leaf. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.
   - Garnish with fresh basil or parsley if desired, and serve over your pasta of choice (tagliatelle or pappardelle are traditional).
