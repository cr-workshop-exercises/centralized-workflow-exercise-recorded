# Potato wedges
*4-6 servings*

## Ingredients 
- Potatoes (1 kg / 4-6 servings)
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Other seasoning of your liking

## Steps
1. Cut the potatoes to wedges
2. Put wedges into a bowl
3. Toss in the oil, salt and other spices, and mix
4. Cook until they are nice and crispy
   - Oven: around 35-40 minutes in 225 degrees
   - Air fryer: around 15-20 minutes in 200 degrees
